1. Corporate architecture and regulatory domicile

Project Delper is a private global digital infrastructure ecosystem with a dual-entity structure in Singapore. Software engineering, ad platform management and data telemetry clearing sit in a Singapore Holding Company (HoldCo).

To comply with ACRA and MAS, investments are separated into an onshore Singapore Variable Capital Company (VCC). The VCC is a passive fund holding fixed-income assets and real estate equities, run by a licensed fund manager.

The ecosystem is anchored by a non-leveraged growth equity injection of $300 million from a Single Family Office (SFO). The whole corporate footprint is chartered to stay permanently private.

4. Global onboarding and verification

User acquisition plugs into existing regional payment systems, mobile data infrastructure and non-invasive software endpoints. No biometric data is collected, stored or processed, in line with GDPR and Illinois BIPA.

  • Mandatory identification: every user completes a digital KYC check tied to local registries to verify identity and redeem rewards.
  • OTP and multi-factor security: accounts and transactions are validated through a real-time, network-delivered OTP. In highly restrictive privacy jurisdictions, a zero-biometric haptic fallback replaces camera-based checks.
  • India: Bharat Bill Payment System (BBPS) and NPCI rails.
  • China: WeChat Mini-Programs.
  • Indonesia: GrabMart and GoMart.
  • Africa: MTN, Airtel and Safaricom/M-Pesa merchant networks.
  • Americas: urban building administrations in Mexico, Pix business codes in Brazil, and Nextdoor in the United States.

5. Sovereign capital reinvestment and GDP expansion

Years 1–28, accumulation. The VCC deploys $180.408 billion a year (the 30% allocation) into senior-secured, 20-year sovereign government infrastructure bonds, benchmarked to emerging-market infrastructure yields. Projects (transit, utilities, logistics corridors) are built by third-party EPC contractors under a BOOT (Build-Own-Operate-Transfer) framework.

Macroeconomic impact. Anchoring these purchases in specific infrastructure tranches is meant to inject money into hard public assets, producing a fiscal multiplier through materials procurement, industrial contracts and logistics efficiency.

Year 29 onwards, roll-over. As tranches mature, principal returns to the VCC treasury and the infrastructure reverts 100% to host nations. This removes long-term maintenance and depreciation liabilities from Delper's ledger. The principal is then reinvested into fresh sovereign bonds.

6. Institutional guardrails and risk shielding

  • Financial shielding: an asset-light data clearinghouse with no loan underwriting, default or credit risk. All consumer credit exposure passes to partner banks.
  • Technology sandboxing: processing runs on hardcoded hardware with one-way entry registries and secure front-end components. AI tools act only as data porters with no executive authority, backed by sandboxed filters on custom front-end chips to prevent logic drift.

7. Viability, ratios and economic moat

  • Breakeven: a Day 1 operating surplus, since subscriptions and media indexing fees are collected upfront, keeping capital burn near zero.
  • DSCR: infinite, due to the non-leveraged, equity-funded structure.
  • Core operating margin: above 95% across SaaS and clearing lines, with inventory, storage and logistics liabilities passed to suppliers via automated SLAs.
  • Tax framework: local indirect and municipal taxes are paid at source. Residual profits return to Singapore, where foreign-sourced dividends and bond coupons are taxed efficiently under the Section 13U Scheme and the single-tier system.
  • Closed-loop moat: returning 30% of revenue as store credits keeps transaction velocity inside the Delper merchant network and ties vendors to HoldCo's predictive intent data.
