Delper India Pilot

Delper = Delivery Personnel

Delper is a buyer-first, cross-platform e-commerce trust layer. It uses independent quality control and escrow banking to protect buyers from counterfeits and fraud, and gives high-quality sellers top visibility.

How it works

  1. Seller listing and biddingSearch results rank factories by verified QC pass rates, not ad spend.
  2. Independent quality checkPartners such as SGS physically inspect goods, targeting risk areas, and issue dispatch codes.
  3. Secure escrowBuyer funds are held by banking partners (Axis Bank is targeted) until delivery.
  4. Release after deliveryFunds are released to the seller once the order is delivered.

Points to verify before relying on this

Fee load on sellers. Seller-paid fees equal about 24% of a $300 order. Sellers' margins, adoption and the pilot's willingness to pay are key unknowns.

Gross profit assumes fees only. The $545M figure works out to exactly the fee per $300 order. It appears to exclude QC, escrow, payment and logistics costs, and assumes an average order of $300.

Dilution. $4.0M on $12.0M pre-money implies a post-money of $16.0M, so the seed investors would hold about 25%.

Partner status. SGS and Axis Bank are described as targets. Confirm signed agreements before treating them as partners.
